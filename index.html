About

My work sits between analysis and application. I start by getting close to the data — cleaning it, questioning it, and looking for the patterns that hold up under scrutiny rather than the ones that look convincing in a chart.

From there I build and evaluate machine learning models, and increasingly spend time on prompt engineering: shaping how language models are instructed so their output is consistent enough to rely on. I care about the last step most — making findings clear to the people who have to make the decision.
